# Licensing and attribution

We keep licensing explicit so that readers can reproduce the work without confusing our contributions with material supplied by others. Different parts of the package have different terms.

## Software

The included [Apache-2.0 licence](LICENSE) covers our software contribution, including the publication-layer verification tools. Frozen source snapshots retain their existing Apache-2.0 notices and scientific bytes. We retain this established licence rather than silently relicense the experimental code.

The GitHub repository was initially created with an MIT template and a short README before this package was added. We preserve that notice in [the initial MIT notice](LICENSE-INITIAL-MIT.txt), scoped to those initial materials. It is not a blanket MIT licence for the experimental software or third-party datasets. The initial commit remains in Git history.

Apache-2.0 includes copyright and patent provisions, notice requirements, warranty disclaimers and limitations of liability. These are licence terms, not a guarantee that any distribution or use is risk-free. See the [official full licence](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

## Documentation and research results

Our original reader guides, explanatory summaries and author-owned experimental results are offered under CC BY 4.0 to the extent that we hold the necessary rights. [REUSE.md](REUSE.md) defines this limited grant and its exclusions. It does not relicense other people's datasets, third-party passages, model-provider material, or the withheld manuscript and publication artwork.

## Third-party data

NetsLab-5GORAN-IDD and 5G-NIDD are third-party datasets with separate terms. The preserved dataset notice records CC BY 4.0 and identifies the creators, source references and transformations. Derived dataset content is not automatically covered by the code licence. See [dataset attribution](code/windows_frozen/DATASET.md).

Raw third-party databases and the private input companion are excluded. Official acquisition instructions and exact input hashes are provided. Some evidence is nevertheless dataset-derived: the Windows archives retain limited 5G-NIDD record fields and computed summaries; campaign provenance includes NetsLab aggregates. These portions are not wholly author-owned observations. Our [standalone third-party notice](data/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identifies their creators, sources, CC BY 4.0 terms and transformations. The companion download README carries the same attribution so it accompanies the separately hosted ZIPs.

Do not redistribute proprietary standards text or complete third-party papers through this repository. Citation locators and policy summaries are sufficient for their role here. The licence grant only covers rights held by its licensor; it cannot supply institutional, co-author, privacy or other third-party permissions that are required separately.

Model responses are supplied research records. Their provider provenance, operator-reported identities and any transformations are documented separately. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement or independent expert certification.

They are not presented as wholly human-authored text or covered by a new blanket grant of provider rights. The model-review guides identify them explicitly. Before a later public-visibility release, the authors must confirm any applicable institutional account and provider distribution requirements.

The [licence verification record](data/DATASET_LICENSE_VERIFICATION.json) records the original provider metadata checked on 28 September 2026. Both sources explicitly declare CC BY 4.0. This provides a documented basis for attributed derived-data sharing, not an absolute legal or privacy guarantee. Institutional and contributor authorization for the authors' own outputs remains a separate release responsibility.
