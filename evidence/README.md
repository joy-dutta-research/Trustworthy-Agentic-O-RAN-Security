# Evidence behind the study

We provide several levels of evidence so readers can inspect the findings first and then reproduce the calculations or make a new run.

- Start in [results](../results/README.md) for the step-by-step interpretation.
- Use [summaries](summaries/README.md) for full saved analyses and [source snapshots](source_snapshots/README.md) for exact executable source bundles.
- Read the [asset guide](ASSET_GUIDE.md) for all six companion ZIPs: what each contains, where to download it, where to place it, and how to produce new evidence if you want to rerun the protocol.
- Use [raw archives](raw_archives/README.md) for detailed historical reanalysis. [History](history/README.md) is optional repair provenance, clearly separated from final outcomes.

The [asset catalogue](RELEASE_ASSETS.json) records exact sizes, hashes and download links for all six companion archives. Our measurement archives are distinct from the original datasets, although some evidence retains attributed dataset-derived fields. Public NetsLab and 5G-NIDD downloads support new runs but cannot replace our historical measurements. Follow [the asset guide](ASSET_GUIDE.md) to obtain the saved evidence and verify its identity.

We preserve source identities and record privacy transformations. Integrity checks establish byte consistency, not scientific improvement by themselves. Read each outcome together with its scope and interpretation.
